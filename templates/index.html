🛡 What are DoD RAI Principles?

The DoD's five AI Ethical Principles (2020): Responsible, Equitable, Traceable, Reliable, Governable. Required for all DoD AI acquisition and deployment per CDAO RAI guidance.

📋 What does this tool assess?

Twenty criteria derived from the five principles. For each criterion, you document implementation status, add notes, and optionally generate a Claude-drafted compliance narrative — the same pattern as NIST 800-53 SSP writing.

⚖ How is Claude used here?

Claude drafts compliance narratives only. The assessment determination (Met / Partial / Not Addressed) is made by a human. Claude never makes the compliance determination — that's the pattern all 7 portfolio projects follow, and the subject this tool makes explicit.
